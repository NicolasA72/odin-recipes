Margarita Recipe

Description

A margarita is a tangy cocktail made with tequila, lime juice, and orange liqueur, often served with a salted rim. It's bright, citrusy, and perfect for summer.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz tequila
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz orange liqueur (Triple Sec or Cointreau)
  • Salt for the rim (optional)
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Rub a lime wedge around the rim of a glass and dip in salt.
  2. Combine tequila, lime juice, and orange liqueur in a shaker with ice.
  3. Shake well until chilled.
  4. Strain into the prepared glass over fresh ice.
  5. Garnish with a lime wheel and serve.
