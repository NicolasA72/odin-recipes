Mojito Recipe

Description

A mojito is a refreshing cocktail made with fresh mint, lime juice, sugar, and rum, topped with sparkling water. It is a classic drink with a bright and refreshing flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 6-8 fresh mint leaves
  • Club soda
  • Ice
