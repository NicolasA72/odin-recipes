Daiquiri Recipe

Description

A daiquiri is a simple, classic cocktail made with rum, lime juice, and simple syrup. It's tart, balanced, and very refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 3/4 oz simple syrup
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Add rum, lime juice, and simple syrup to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously until well chilled.
  3. Double strain into a chilled coupe or cocktail glass.
  4. Garnish with a lime twist or wheel. Serve immediately.
